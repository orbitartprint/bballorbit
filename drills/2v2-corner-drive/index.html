    2v2 Corner Drive

    Competitive shooting under pressure.

    Main Goal

    Force a paint touch and create a high-quality finish or kick-out against help defense.

    Setup

    1. The ball handler and primary defender start in the corner, both outside the 3-point line.

    2. A second defender waits under the basket, ready to help as the attacker enters the lane.

    3. A second attacker stands in the opposite corner, positioned for a kick-out pass.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The ball handler O1 must penetrate and achieve a paint touch before attempting a finish. The second defender X2 comes for a help defense.

    Phase 2

    Once the paint touch occurs, the drill becomes fully live 2v2.

    Phase 3

    Constraints

    1. Award extra points for made 3-pointers after the kick-out.

    2. Second defender may only help from inside the paint — no early cheating.

    3. Play in a reduced space (below FT line extended) for tighter spacing decisions.
