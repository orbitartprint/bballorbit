    Chair Chase Shooting

    A competitive shooting drill that creates rushed, game-like shots while forcing the shooter to make a decision.

    Main Goal

    Help players take game-like shots under pressure by reading the recovering defender and deciding whether to shoot or attack the basket.

    Setup

    One offensive player starts with a ball at the top, while one defender starts opposite him. Between them, place one or two chairs or cones.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The defender starts the drill by choosing one side and running around the chairs.

    2. The ball handler reacts to the defender’s movement and creates enough space to take a shot before the defender can recover.

    3. This drill focuses on rushed, game-like shooting situations. The shot should feel slightly uncomfortable, but still realistic.

    Phase 2

    You can start with a basic pull-up jumper from outside the paint or outside the three-point line.

    Phase 3

    By adding a second offensive player, you can also create a catch-and-shoot situation.

    Phase 4

    To keep the decision-making element, allow the shooter to drive if the shot is not open.
