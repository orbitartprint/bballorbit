    1v1 High-Five Scramble

    Win the ball, get to your feet, and finish against a chasing defender.

    Main Goal

    Develop quick reactions and body control while training players to turn a loose-ball win into an immediate attack and finish under pressure.

    Setup

    1. Two players face each other at half court in a push-up position.

    2. Place one ball between them on the half-court line, within reach of both players.

    3. Designate the basket they will attack.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The players give each other a high-five to start the drill. Both then compete for the ball while remaining in the push-up position.

    Phase 2

    1. As soon as one player gains clear possession, that player picks up the ball, gets to their feet, and dribbles toward the designated basket for a layup.

    2. The other player immediately gets up and becomes the defender, chasing back to contest the finish.

    3. Play until a basket, defensive rebound, or turnover. Reset at half court and repeat.

    Variations

    1. Different starting positions: Start seated or kneeling instead of in a push-up position.

    2. Balance battle: Both players start standing on one leg. After the high-five, they compete for the ball while maintaining their balance. Putting the raised foot down or falling over loses the contest; the other player gains possession and attacks.
