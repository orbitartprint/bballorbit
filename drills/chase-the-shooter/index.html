    Shooting

    Chase the Shooter

    Create separation and shoot under pressure.

    Main Goal

    Train shooters to create separation, sprint into space, and knock down shots while a trailing defender applies late pressure.

    Setup

    A shooter and defender start at designated cone-marked positions in the half court, with the shooter holding a small head start.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The drill begins when the shooter takes off toward a shooting spot.

    2. Upon arrival, the shooter takes a pull-up jumper while being chased by the defender, who tries to contest late.

    Phase 2

    1. Another player or coach acts as a passer, creating a catch-and-shoot situation.

    2. Vary starting positions to change angles, distance, and level of advantage.
