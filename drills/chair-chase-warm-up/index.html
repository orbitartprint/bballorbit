    Chair Chase Warm-Up

    A simple and competitive warm-up drill that forces players to react, move quickly, and make fast decisions.

    Main Goal

    Help players warm up in a competitive and game-like way while reacting to the defender’s movement and making quick decisions with the ball.

    Setup

    One offensive player starts with a ball at the top, while one defender starts opposite him. Between them, place one or two chairs or cones.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The defender starts the drill by choosing one side and running around the chairs. The goal for the defender is to tag the ball handler before he finishes.

    Phase 2

    This drill is designed as a warm-up, so the focus is on movement, reaction, ball handling, and decision-making rather than scoring.

    Phase 3

    Phase 4

    Constraints

    1. Use one chair for a quicker read or two chairs to create more space and movement.

    2. Move the chairs closer together to reduce space or farther apart to increase space.

    3. The ball handler can start stationary or already dribbling.

    4. The defender can also start with a ball and must keep the dribble alive while trying to tag the offensive player.

    5. Change the starting positions of the players and chairs to create different movement patterns.
