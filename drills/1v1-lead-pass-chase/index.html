    1v1 Lead Pass Chase

    Create separation, receive the pass in stride, and finish before the defender recovers.

    Main Goal
    Train receivers to create an advantage with an explosive cut, while helping passers time and place a lead pass for a finish under pressure.

    Setup

    1. O1 starts with the ball near one sideline at half court.

    2. O2 starts near the opposite sideline at half court, guarded by X2.

    3. Both baskets are available, depending on O2’s chosen direction.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. O2 starts the drill by choosing a direction and sprinting toward either basket. X2 reacts immediately and tries to stay with O2, deny the pass, and contest the finish.

    2. O1 reads the cut and may take up to two dribbles to improve the passing angle. O1 then delivers a lead pass into O2’s path.

    Phase 2

    O2 looks to create separation, receive the ball in stride, and finish with a layup before X2 recovers. The pass should lead O2 toward the basket without forcing a stop or a change of running line.

    Play until a score, defensive rebound, or turnover. Reset at half court and rotate roles. Encourage O2 to attack both directions across repetitions.

    Constraints

    • One-dribble passer: Limit O1 to one dribble before passing.

    • Direct finish: O2 must finish without an additional dribble after the catch.

    • Adjust defensive pressure: Change X2’s starting distance or position relative to O2 to create different passing windows.
