    2v2 Sandwich

    Quick-start 2v2 that forces spacing + defensive communication from a tight alignment.

    Main Goal

    Train immediate spacing and advantage creation on offense while forcing defenders to communicate, match up, and contain the ball in a live 2v2 situation.

    Setup

    Line up 4 players on the three-point line in one straight line. The two middle players are offense (O1 with the ball, O2 without the ball). The two outside players are defense.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The rep starts as soon as the designated trigger player moves (default: the ball-handler). Once the trigger moves, all four players are live and it becomes a normal 2v2.

    2. Offense must instantly create proper spacing (avoid standing on the same line / same lane), play with pace, and look to create an advantage with cuts, re-spacing, or screens.

    Phase 2

    Play until a score, stop, or defensive rebound. Reset quickly and rotate positions/roles.

    Phase 3

    Constraints

    1. Trigger variations: Start on the movement of (a) ball-handler, (b) offensive player without the ball, (c) X1, or (d) X2. (Pick one and run 3–5 reps.)

    2. Finishing constraint: Offense must finish with a layup (no jump shots).

    3. pacing rule: On the catch or after the first drive, the off-ball offensive player must 're-space' to a clear window (corner/slot/dunker spot) — no standing within 2 meters of the ball.

    4. Screens allowed: Offense may set on-ball screens.

    5. Ball-handler has max 2-3 dribbles before a shot or a pass.

    6. Time pressure: Shot must happen within 5 seconds after the trigger movement.
