    2v1 Elbow Attack

    Attack from the elbows and create a finish with no more than one pass.

    Main Goal
    Help attackers read a single defender and decide whether to finish or use one pass to create a better scoring opportunity.

    Setup

    1. O1 and O2 start at the elbows, one on each side.

    2. A coach stands at the top with a ball.

    3. X1 starts between the attackers, facing the coach.

    4. Additional attackers wait off the court, ready to enter in pairs.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The coach passes to either attacker. As soon as the pass is delivered, both attackers and X1 are live.

    Phase 2

    1. The receiver can attack the basket off the dribble or pass to the teammate, depending on the defender’s position. The attackers may make a maximum of one pass between them.

    2. The off-ball attacker moves to provide a clear passing option.

    Phase 3

    After the single pass has been used, the receiver must finish without passing back. In the base version, only layups are allowed.

    Phase 4

    After the shot, the shooter becomes the defender. The other attacker and the previous defender step out, and two new attackers enter at the elbows.

    Return the ball to the coach and repeat.

    Constraints

    1. Allow jump shots: Let attackers shoot as well as drive, adding the decision of whether to take the open shot or attack the basket.
