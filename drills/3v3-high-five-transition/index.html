    Transition Defense

    3v3 High-Five Transition

    Push the break while fresh defenders enter through a high-five exchange.

    Main Goal
    Develop quick transitions and defensive communication, while helping attackers exploit an advantage before incoming defenders get organized.

    Setup

    1. Start with live 3v3 in the half court.

    2. At least three additional players wait just outside the court at half court.

    3. Use the full court and one ball. A minimum of nine players is required.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    Play normal 3v3 until the defense gains possession through a rebound, steal, or turnover. That team immediately attacks the opposite basket.

    Phase 2

    1. The former attackers become the outgoing defenders. They sprint to half court, where each player high-fives a waiting teammate. Each waiting player may enter as a new defender immediately after receiving the high-five.

    2. The outgoing players step off and join the waiting line.

    Phase 3

    1. The incoming defenders communicate, match up, and try to stop the advancing offense.

    2. The attackers push the ball and look to score before the fresh defense organizes.

    Phase 4

    Whenever possession changes, repeat the same exchange and attack the opposite basket. After a made basket, inbound quickly and continue the rotation.

    Constraints

    1. Keep teams together: With 12 or more players, divide into two teams. One team’s substitutes wait on the left sideline at half court, the other team’s on the right. Players exchange only with their own teammates.

    2. No backcourt passes: After any necessary inbound, advance the ball across half court by dribbling. Passing is allowed in the frontcourt.

    3. Shot selection: Only layups or three-pointers are allowed.

    4. 8-second possessions: Require a shot within 8 seconds of gaining possession.
