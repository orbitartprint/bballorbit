    3v3 Continuous Attack

    Attack instantly, switch roles, and match up before the next possession gets away.

    Main Goal

    Train quick defensive recognition and communication after a possession ends, while encouraging attackers to exploit an unsettled defense.

    Setup

    1. Start with three attackers and three defenders in the half court.

    2. A third trio waits at half court with a second ball, ready to attack.

    3. Use at least nine players and two balls.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    Begin with live 3v3 half-court play.

    Phase 2

    As soon as the defense secures possession through a rebound, steal, or retrieving the ball after a made basket, the waiting trio immediately enters with its own ball and attacks the same basket.

    Phase 3

    The previous defenders leave the court with the old ball and move to half court, where they prepare to enter as the next attacking trio.

    Phase 4

    1. The former offensive team becomes the new defense. These players must react immediately, communicate their matchups, and stop the incoming attackers.

    2. Continue this rotation without pausing between possessions. Incoming attackers look to use the advantage before the defense organizes, while the new defenders prioritize stopping the ball, protecting the basket, and finding matchups.

    Phase 5

    Constraints

    1. 0.5-second decisions: On every catch, the receiver must shoot, pass, or begin a drive within 0.5 seconds. Hesitation beyond that results in a turnover and triggers the next rotation.

    2. Shot selection: Only layups or three-pointers following a kick-out pass are allowed.

    3. Different entry angles: Vary the waiting trio’s starting positions along half court to change the direction of the attack.
