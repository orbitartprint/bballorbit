    Corner Race Dodgeball

    Win the race to the ball, then hit your opponent or escape to the far baseline.

    Main Goal
    Develop acceleration, agility, and quick reactions as players switch from racing for possession to chasing or escaping.

    Setup

    1. Two players start in opposite corners at the same end of the court.

    2. Place a chair at the top of the three-point arc with a soft dodgeball or foam ball on it. Do not use a basketball.

    3. Use the opposite baseline as the runner’s finish line.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    On the coach’s signal, both players sprint from their corners toward the chair, staying outside the three-point line.

    Phase 2

    1. The first player to take possession of the ball becomes the thrower. The other player immediately becomes the runner and tries to reach the opposite baseline without being hit.

    2. The thrower chases and looks for an opportunity to hit the runner with the ball, aiming below shoulder height.

    3. The thrower earns one point for a successful hit before the runner reaches the opposite baseline. The runner earns one point by crossing the baseline without being hit.

    4. Reset the ball on the chair and repeat. Switch starting corners between repetitions.
