    Transition Defense

    2v1 + 1 Full Court

    Attack the early advantage, read the recovering defense, and transition immediately.

    Main Goal

    Help players exploit a temporary numbers advantage before help arrives, then react quickly to changing roles in continuous full-court play.

    Setup

    1. Two attackers (O1 and O2) start with one ball against one defender (X1) in the backcourt.

    2. A second defender (X2) waits at the half-court line.

    3. Use the full court, with the center circle as X2’s recovery checkpoint.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. O1 and O2 advance toward the opposite basket against X1, looking to use the initial 2v1 advantage.

    2. As soon as the first attacker crosses half court, X2 enters but must touch the center circle before defending. The possession becomes live 2v2 once X2 joins.

    Phase 2

    Play until a shot or defensive stop.

    Phase 3

    The attacker who last touched the ball — by shooting or committing the turnover — steps out and waits at half court as the next delayed defender.

    Phase 4

    1. The remaining attacker immediately becomes the transition defender and sprints back.

    2. The two former defenders secure possession, inbound after a made basket if needed, and attack the opposite basket.

    3. When the first new attacker crosses half court, the waiting defender (X3) enters, touches the center circle, and joins the defense. Repeat continuously with the same rotation.

    Phase 5

    Constraints

    • Change the recovery route: The delayed defender must touch a designated cone instead of the center circle.

    • Paint touch required: Offense must get a paint touch before attempting to score.

    • Two-dribble limit: Each attacker may use a maximum of two dribbles in the frontcourt per possession.

    • Shot selection: Only layups or catch-and-shoot three-pointers are allowed.
