    Tag & Escape Warm-Up (1v1)

    Quick reactions, agility, and evasion under pressure.

    Main Goal

    Improve first-step quickness and evasive movement while training the defender to react explosively and protect space.

    Setup

    1. One offensive player (the runner) starts on the baseline.

    2. A defender (the tagger) stands at the free-throw line, holding a ball with both hands.

    3. Two cones are placed to the left and right of the defender.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The drill begins when the runner initiates movement. The runner must try to reach either cone without being tagged by the tagger’s ball.

    2. The tagger may only tag using the ball held with two hands — no reaching or swinging with arms allowed.

    Phase 2

    Constraints

    1. The runner must dribble while escaping (adds complexity and ball control).

    2. Vary the distance and positioning of the cones to change difficulty.

    3. Move the tagger’s starting position (wider, narrower, angled).

    4. The runner must perform at least one cross over (behind the back / between the legs).
