    2v2 Rolling Break

    Continuous 2-on-2 Transition Drill.

    Main Goal

    Improve transition awareness, quick defensive reactions, and offensive flow in small-sided situations.

    Setup

    1. Start with two offensive players and two defenders playing live 2-on-2.

    2. Two additional players wait out of bounds on each side at the free-throw line extended.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    Phase 2

    1. When the defense gains possession or the offense scores, both offensive players step off the court.

    2. One defender picks up the ball, ...

    Phase 3

    ... and immediately transitions to offense, attacking the opposite basket.

    The two sideline players quickly step in as the new defenders — keeping the drill continuous.

    Phase 4

    This sequence repeats back and forth, emphasizing quick transitions and communication.

    Phase 5

    Phase 6

    Constraints

    1. Vary the starting spots of the waiting players, e.g. one player at the baseline and the other player at the midline.

    2. Adjust the court size — for example, play only on one side of the floor.

    3. Limit the time allowed for each offensive possession.

    4. Disallow passes within the backcourt to increase tempo and decision-making speed.
