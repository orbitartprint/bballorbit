    1v1 Rock, Paper, Scissors

    Win the ball, react quickly, and turn the result into a live 1v1.

    Main Goal
    Develop quick reactions and immediate transitions into offense or defense, with an emphasis on attacking before the defender gets set.

    Setup

    1. Two players stand side by side at half court, with one ball on the floor between them.

    2. Designate the basket they will attack.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The players face each other and play rock, paper, scissors. If they tie, they play again.

    Phase 2

    1. The winner picks up the ball and immediately attacks the designated basket. The loser becomes the defender and reacts as soon as the result is clear.

    2. Play live 1v1 until a score, defensive rebound, or turnover. Reset at half court and repeat.

    Constraints / Variations

    1. Layups only: The attacker must finish with a layup.

    2. Time pressure: The attacker must attempt a shot within five seconds of picking up the ball.

    3. Either basket: Allow the winner to choose which basket to attack, forcing the defender to react to the direction.
