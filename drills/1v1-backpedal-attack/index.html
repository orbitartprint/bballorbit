    1v1 Backpedal Attack

    Retreat to the free-throw line, then change direction and attack.

    Main Goal

    Help ball handlers maintain control while dribbling backward, then read the defender and create an advantage when changing direction toward the basket.

    Setup

    1. One offensive player (O1) starts with the ball inside the paint, facing the basket.

    2. One defender (X1) stands in front of O1, facing the ball handler.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. O1 dribbles backward toward the free-throw line.

    2. O1 must reach the free-throw line, and X1 must also place at least one foot on the line either on the left or right side of the ball handler.

    Phase 2

    1. As soon as O1 touches the free-throw line with a foot, O1 can change direction and attack the basket.

    2. X1 must also touch the free-throw line with at least one foot before defending.

    3. Play until a score, defensive rebound, or turnover. Reset inside the paint and switch roles.

    Constraints

    1. Crossover required: O1 must perform a crossover at the free-throw line before attacking the basket.

    2. Dribble limit: Allow a maximum of three dribbles after the drill becomes live to encourage a decisive attack.
