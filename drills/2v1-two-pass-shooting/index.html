    2v1 Two-Pass Shooting

    Read the closeout, find the open shooter, and finish within two passes.

    Main Goal
    Help shooters recognize when to shoot or move the ball against a recovering defender, while developing quick decisions and readiness on the catch.

    Setup

    1. Two shooters (O1 and O2) start at designated shooting spots.

    2. One defender (X1) starts inside the court, ready to close out to either shooter.

    3. A passer or coach stands in the paint with a ball.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The passer starts the drill by passing to either shooter.

    Phase 2

    1. X1 immediately closes out to contest.

    2. The receiver reads the closeout and decides whether to shoot or pass to the other shooter. X1 reacts to each pass and tries to contest the next receiver’s shot.

    Phase 3

    1. The shooters may make a maximum of two passes between them after the entry pass. Either player can shoot earlier, but after the second pass, the receiver must shoot.

    2. Both shooters stay ready to catch and shoot, moving the ball quickly when the defender takes away the shot.

    3. Reset after the shot and rotate roles.

    Phase 4

    Constraints

    1. Allow the drive: Shooters may also attack the basket when the defender closes out aggressively. After the second pass, the receiver must shoot or drive.

    2. Add rim protection: In the driving variation, the passer becomes a stationary second defender under the basket after delivering the entry pass. This defender may contest with raised arms but must keep their feet planted and may not jump.
