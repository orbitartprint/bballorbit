    Shooting

    Closeout Chase Shooting

    Create separation and shoot under pressure.

    Main Goal

    Help players create enough separation for an open shot while improving shooting under pressure and closeout recognition.

    Setup

    1. One offensive player starts with the ball at the half-court line, while one defender starts underneath the basket on the baseline.

    2. Place one cone at each elbow.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The defender starts the drill by running around either cone before trying to recover and contest the shot.

    2. As soon as the defender moves, the offensive player attacks the available space and tries to create as much distance as possible before taking the shot.

    3. The shooter must read the defender’s path, find open space, and get the shot off before the defender can recover.

    Phase 2

    For a catch-and-shoot variation, the offensive player starts without the ball and receives a pass from a coach or teammate before taking the shot.
