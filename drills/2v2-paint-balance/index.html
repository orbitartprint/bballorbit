    2v2 Paint Balance

    Halfcourt 2v2 with defensive inside-outside rule and offensive spacing constraints.

    Main Goal

    Train offensive spacing and quick advantage creation under time pressure, while forcing the defense to constantly balance paint protection and perimeter pressure.

    Setup

    1. Play a live 2v2 in the half court.

    2. Defensive rule: One defender must always be inside the paint (zone), and the other defender must always be outside the paint.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    If one defender steps into the paint, the other defender must immediately leave it. They must constantly adjust their positioning to maintain inside-outside balance.

    Phase 2

    The offense plays normal 2v2 but must recognize which defender is protecting the paint and which one is pressuring outside.

    Phase 3

    The goal for the offense is to create quick advantages before the defense can properly rotate and rebalance.

    Phase 4

    Play until a score, stop, or shot-clock violation, then switch roles.

    Phase 5

    Constraints

    1. Offensive half restriction: One offensive player may only operate on the left side of the court, the other only on the right side (no crossing the middle).

    2. Catch rule: Offensive players may only receive the ball outside the three-point line.

    3. Scoring system: Three-pointers = 3 points; any 2-point field goal = 1 point (reward spacing and outside shooting).

    4. Time constraint: Offense has 6–8 seconds to complete each possession.

    5. Dribble limit: Max 3 dribbles per player to increase ball movement and decision speed.
