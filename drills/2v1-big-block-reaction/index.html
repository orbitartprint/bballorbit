    2v1 Big Block Reaction

    React to the pass and finish before the defender can contest.

    Main Goal
    Help attackers finish quickly under pressure while training the defender to locate the ball, react, and contest an unexpected close-range shot.

    Setup

    1. O1 and O2 start at the low blocks, one on each side of the paint.

    2. A coach stands at the free-throw line with a ball.

    3. X1 starts between the attackers with their back to the coach.

    4. Additional attackers wait off the court, ready to enter in pairs.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The coach passes to either O1 or O2 without announcing the target.

    Phase 2

    As soon as the pass is delivered, X1 reacts and moves to contest the receiver’s shot. The receiver must finish without passing, using positioning and a quick release to score under pressure.

    Phase 3

    After the shot, the shooter becomes the defender. The previous defender and the non-shooting attacker step out, and two new attackers take the block positions.

    Return the ball to the coach and repeat, varying the target and timing of the pass.

    Constraints

    1. Allow a dribble: Give the receiver a maximum of one dribble to improve the finishing angle or get past the contest. Passing remains prohibited.

    2. No backboard: All finishes must go directly into the basket without using the backboard.
