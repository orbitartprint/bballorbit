    4v4 Scramble Ball

    Win the loose ball, choose a basket, and attack before the defense organizes.

    Main Goal
    Develop quick recognition of offensive and defensive roles in an unpredictable situation, with an emphasis on decisive attacks, spacing, and defensive communication.

    Setup

    1. Divide eight players into two teams of four.

    2. All players line up along the half-court line.

    3. A coach stands out of bounds at half court with a ball. Both baskets are available.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The coach starts the drill by throwing the ball to an unpredictable location on the court. All eight players react and compete to secure possession.

    Phase 2

    1. The team that wins the ball becomes the offense and chooses which basket to attack. Teammates immediately spread out and create passing options to exploit the unsettled defense.

    2. The other team transitions to defense, communicating quickly to stop the ball, protect the chosen basket, and find matchups.

    3. Play until a score, defensive stop, or turnover. Reset at half court and repeat with a different throw.

    Constraints

    1. Vary the throw: Send the ball toward a deep corner, the free-throw line, or into space with a long bounce to create different recovery situations.

    2. Immediate direction: The offense must choose its target basket within 0.5 seconds of securing possession.

    3. Shot selection: Only layups or catch-and-shoot three-pointers are allowed.

    4. Reduced space: Run the drill in the half court with one designated basket. Position the coach out of bounds under the basket and throw the ball into the playing area.
