    2v2 Chair Chase

    A competitive 2v2 drill that creates an immediate advantage and forces all four players to make quick decisions.

    Main Goal

    Help players recognize and use an early advantage in a live 2v2 situation while improving spacing, communication, and decision-making on both sides of the ball.

    Setup

    1. One offensive player starts with the ball below the first defender, while a second offensive player is positioned at the top.

    2. Place one chair or cone between O1 and X1.

    3. A second defender starts underneath the basket.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    The first defender starts the drill by choosing one side and running around the chair. As soon as the defender moves, the drill is live. The offense must immediately read the advantage and decide whether to attack, pass, cut, or use the second offensive player.

    Constraints

    1. Limit the offense to a maximum number of dribbles to encourage passing and quick decisions.

    2. Require the offense to attack within a set time limit.

    3. Award bonus points for a score created directly from the initial advantage.

    4. Require both offensive players to touch the ball before a shot.

    5. Vary the starting positions of the second offensive player and the help defender.
