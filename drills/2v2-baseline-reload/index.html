    2v2 Baseline Reload

    Continuous 2-on-2 Baseline Transition Drill.

    Main Goal

    Develop fast transition reactions, continuous play, and defensive communication under pressure.

    Setup

    1. Start with two offensive players and two defenders playing live 2-on-2.

    2. Two additional offensive players wait behind the baseline on each side of the floor.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    Limit each possession to a single shot attempt to keep the tempo high.

    Phase 2

    After a (missed) shot or turnover, one of the waiting players behind the baseline grabs the ball and attacks the other basket with their teammate.

    Phase 3

    The former offensive players instantly switch to defense, while the original defenders step off and wait behind the baseline for their next turn.

    Phase 4

    The drill continues in a fast, back-and-forth rhythm with constant possession changes and no breaks.

    Constraints

    1. Vary the starting spots of the waiting players.

    2. Adjust the court size — for example, play only on one side of the floor.

    3. Limit the time allowed for each offensive possession.

    4. Disallow passes within the backcourt to increase tempo and decision-making speed.

    5. Offensive player who last touched the ball must touch sideline before defending.
