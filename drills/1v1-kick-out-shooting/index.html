    1v1 Kick-Out Shooting

    Competitive shooting under pressure.

    Main Goal

    Develop shooting consistency under pressure and decision making.

    Setup

    Start with a 1-on-1 situation and one additional shooter positioned anywhere around the arc.

    Drill Phases

    Phase 1

    1. The ball handler attacks the basket and must get at least one foot into the paint before making any pass.

    2. After driving into the paint, the ball handler kicks the ball out to the perimeter shooter.

    Phase 2

    The defender immediately follows the pass and closes out hard to contest the shot.

    Phase 3

    1. Once the pass is made, the ball handler sprints behind the arc to become a passing option.

    2. The shooter has the option to shoot or to pass.

    Phase 4

    The initial ball handler now has the option to pass or shoot again.

    Constraints

    1. Players must vary the starting positions after each rep.

    2. Adjust the scoring system to highlight whatever skill you want to focus on.

    3. Allow or disallow penetrations.

    4. Limit the number of passes or dribbles.
